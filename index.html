The Shop

A traditional chair on St Peter’s.

A F Barber Shop sits on the long parade of St Peter’s Avenue in Cleethorpes — the row of independents that runs back from the seafront. It’s a small, independent men’s shop: a quiet chair, a sharp set of clippers, and the kind of finish you only get from someone who’s done it a thousand times.

Pop in, sit down, and we’ll sort you out. If the chair’s busy, ring ahead — most days we can fit you in within the hour.
