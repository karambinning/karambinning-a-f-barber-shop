§ 01 — Where

On St Peter's Avenue.

An independent men's barber shop on Cleethorpes' main pedestrianised avenue — a five-minute walk from the seafront.

The avenue is the town's long-running high street: cafés, jewellers, charity shops, the odd record bin. A F sits at number twenty-one — old-school red chair, mirrors, the clatter of clippers, conversation on the day's football.

Cuts for grown men. Cuts for kids. Beards squared off properly. No app, no booking system — open the door and take a seat.
